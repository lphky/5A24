TACTICAL BRIEFING & RULES

🎯 1. FRONTLINE SHOOTER MECHANICS

German soldiers spawn on the left panel. Click or tap to shoot them! Clear them before active troops reach 15.

🛡️ 2. REINFORCEMENT TIMELINE & ARMOR

  • 0:00 – 2:00 (Initial): Basic Recon Soldiers (2 HP, Static).
  • 2:00 (Wave 1): Tactical Armored Soldiers (3 HP 🛡️, Patrol Movement).
  • 5:00 (Wave 2): Heavy Titanium Soldiers (4 HP 🔰, +50% Speed).

📜 3. RESISTANCE AGENT & INTEL RECON

Resistance Spies (🕵️) occasionally appear (~1.5% spawn chance). Leave them unclicked for 10s to gain +1 Intel. Press `[E]` or click Intel to activate 3x3 Radar Scan on the minefield!

📱 4. MOBILE FLAG MODE & CONTROLS

Tap `DIG / FLAG MODE` (or press `[F]`) to toggle tap-flagging mode. On touch devices, holding a tile for >350ms also places/removes flags.

🚨 5. AIRSTRIKE THREAT

If active German troops reach 15, a Stuka Airstrike will destroy your command post immediately!
